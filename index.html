HOW TO PLAY

📷

Step 1 — Setup Camera

Allow webcam access when prompted. Position yourself so your hand is clearly visible. No data leaves your device.

✋

Step 2 — Open Your Palm

Hold your open hand flat in front of the camera. The giant virtual hand will mirror your movements in 3D.

💨

Step 3 — Move Fast

Move your hand quickly across your body. The faster you move, the more powerful your slap!

👊

Step 4 — Slap!

Swing sideways fast to trigger a slap. Land hits in quick succession to build COMBO multipliers for massive damage.

💀

Step 5 — Defeat the Boss!

Drain the boss HP bar before the timer hits zero. Win to unlock the next, tougher boss. Beat all 5 to become Slap King!
